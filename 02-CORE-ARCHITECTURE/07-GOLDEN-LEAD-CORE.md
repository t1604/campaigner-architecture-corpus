0. PURPOSE


מסמך זה מבצע Reconciliation בין Product Truth, טיוטות Golden Lead ההיסטוריות, GOLDEN-LEAD-KNOWLEDGE-AND-TRANSFERABILITY-001, Business Economics, Sales/Lead Handling, Measurement, Hillstrom/GAP-021 וה-Professional Knowledge Foundation.


המטרה היא להגדיר את אמת-התחום של Golden Lead בלי להפוך אותה לציון, targeter, מנוע allocation או הסבר סיבתי.


1. ROLE OF THE CORE


Golden Lead Core מחזיק את אמת הערך וההתאמה העסקית של ליד/הזדמנות עבור Business × Offering × current context מסוימים, ואת ההבחנה בין:
- מה העסק רוצה;
- מה מתאים ובעל פוטנציאל ערך;
- מה ניתן לצפות/לחזות;
- מה העסק מסוגל לממש;
- ומה התממש בפועל.


ה-Core נותן ל-Campainer מצפן רב-ממדי שמונע מ-CPL, volume, click, close או score מקומי להפוך לבדם להגדרת הצלחה.


2. GOLDEN LEAD IS RELATIONAL, NOT A PERSON TRAIT


Golden Lead אינו תכונה קבועה של אדם.


הערך תלוי לפחות ב:
Contact/Account × Need/Buying Situation × Offering/Campaign Offer × Business/User Goals × Time × Serviceability/Capacity × Economics × Decision Context.


אותו אדם יכול להיות בעל ערך גבוה לעסק אחד, נמוך לאחר, מתאים ל-Offering אחד ולא לאחר, או מתאים אך לא בשל כרגע.


3. ENTITY SEPARATION


CONTACT ≠ ACCOUNT ≠ NEED ≠ OFFERING ≠ OPPORTUNITY ≠ LEAD INSTANCE ≠ ROUTE.


ב-B2B:
PERSON/CONTACT ≠ ACCOUNT ≠ DECISION UNIT ≠ OPPORTUNITY.


שינוי Contact, Offering, timing או Route אינו משכתב את ההיסטוריה של המופע המקורי; הוא משנה את מצב ההזדמנות או יוצר מופע מקושר בהתאם לארכיטקטורה העתידית.


4. CORE PRINCIPLE — MULTIDIMENSIONAL TRUTH, NOT ONE SCORE


Golden Lead Truth אינו מספר אחד.


Candidate truth dimensions/families:


A. FIT / ELIGIBILITY
האם הצורך/הלקוח/החשבון מתאימים ל-Offering שהעסק באמת יכול לספק.


B. DESIRED / STRATEGIC VALUE
האם זה סוג הלקוח/העבודה/החשבון שהעסק רוצה יותר ממנו, גם כאשר הערך הזה אינו זהה לרווח חשבונאי קצר-טווח.


C. ECONOMIC VALUE
הערך העסקי האפשרי לאחר contribution, relevant costs, handling, cost-to-serve, payment/cancellation/rework, capacity/opportunity cost ויחסי עתיד כאשר רלוונטיים ומדידים.


D. BUYER / OPPORTUNITY STATE
Need, Intent, Readiness, Urgency, Timing and other buyer-state evidence may affect current potential/actionability, but must not be collapsed into business desirability or treated as latent truth without evidence.


E. SERVICEABILITY / CAPACITY FIT
האם העסק מסוגל ורוצה לשרת את ההזדמנות היטב בהקשר הזמן הנוכחי, כולל geography, capacity, delivery/fulfillment and material operational constraints.


F. DECISION ACCESS — WHEN RELEVANT
האם קיימת גישה/מסלול סביר לפונקציות הנדרשות להחלטה; non-final contact is not automatically bad lead.


G. CONFIDENCE / EVIDENCE STATE
כל מסקנה על הממדים נושאת Evidence, Scope, Freshness ו-Confidence. Confidence אינו ממד ערך ואינו “מעלה/מוריד את הלקוח”; הוא אומר כמה אנחנו יודעים.


המשפחות המדויקות דורשות Owner review. אין לקבע מספר ממדים רק מפני שטיוטה היסטורית השתמשה במספר מסוים.


5. DESIRED TRAIT ≠ PREDICTIVE SIGNAL


Owner/business preference כגון:
“אני רוצה לקוחות שמשלמים בזמן”,
“אני רוצה פרויקטים מסוג X”,
“אני לא רוצה עבודה מסוג Y”
יכולה להיות Local Business Truth לגיטימית.


היא אינה הופכת אוטומטית למשתנה שניתן לזהות לפני הפנייה, ל-targeting attribute או ל-predictor.


Campainer must separate:
DESIRED OUTCOME TRAIT
≠ OBSERVABLE SIGNAL
≠ PREDICTIVE MODEL OUTPUT
≠ TARGETING/ROUTING PERMISSION.


6. POTENTIAL QUALITY ≠ BUSINESS REALIZATION ≠ REALIZED OUTCOME


זהו עיקרון מרכזי מועמד ל-Core.


POTENTIAL / GOLDEN QUALITY
מתאר את ערכה והתאמתה של ההזדמנות לפי הראיות על ההזדמנות והעסק.


BUSINESS REALIZATION CAPABILITY
מתאר את יכולת העסק בפועל לקלוט, להגיב, ליצור קשר, לבצע Qualification, follow-up, meeting/proposal, fulfillment וכדומה.


REALIZED OUTCOME
מתאר מה קרה בפועל: won/lost, contribution, collection, cancellation/rework, fulfillment, repeat/referral וכו' לפי Scope/Horizon.


כשל במענה או Sales Handling אינו מוכיח בדיעבד שהליד היה באיכות פוטנציאל נמוכה.
הוא עשוי להסביר פער בין Potential לבין Realized Outcome.


ראיה חדשה על הליד עצמו כן יכולה לשנות את הערכת הפוטנציאל.


7. FOUR ADJACENT OBJECTS THAT MUST NOT COLLAPSE INTO GOLDEN LEAD


המחקר וה-Hillstrom reconciliation מחייבים הפרדה בין:


1. CUSTOMER / OPPORTUNITY VALUE
הערך/הרצויות לעסק.


2. BASELINE OUTCOME PROPENSITY
הסבירות לתוצאה ללא intervention מסוים.


3. INCREMENTAL MARKETING OPPORTUNITY
השינוי הצפוי כתוצאה מ-intervention מסוים, תמיד scoped ל-State × Intervention × Outcome × Horizon.


4. ACCESS / PRESENCE / RETRIEVABILITY NEED
הצורך של הקונה להיחשף, לגלות, לזכור או להכניס את הספק ל-consideration.


אלה אינם אותו דבר ואינם נגזרים אוטומטית זה מזה.


High Value does not imply high incremental paid-media response.
Low uplift does not imply low customer value or “do not expose”.
High baseline propensity does not establish marketing causality.
Access need is not measured by known-customer uplift evidence.


8. INTENT / READINESS BOUNDARY


Intent is decision-relevant but must remain semantically separate from:
- observed behavior;
- readiness;
- urgency;
- fit;
- value;
- realized purchase.


Open Owner/Core question:
האם Intent הוא dimension פנימי של Golden Potential, או Buyer-State input שמזין את הערכת Potential/Actionability. אין לסגור זאת בשקט.


Regardless of placement, no click/lead/stated desire becomes “true intent” automatically.


9. ECONOMICS BOUNDARY


Golden Lead consumes local economics; it does not invent them.


Revenue ≠ Contribution.
Average ≠ Marginal.
Booked/Won ≠ Collected/Realized.
LTV is optional and model/horizon/data dependent.
Capacity can alter marginal value and create opportunity cost.
Desired/Strategic Value can conflict with short-run contribution.


All numeric economics remain Local Business Truth/Calibration.


10. QUANTITY / PACE / SUSTAINABILITY


Product Truth defines the North Star as Golden Lead and the business’s ability to derive a sustainable outcome.


Therefore Golden Lead cannot be interpreted as “find the highest-scoring individual only.”
The system must support decisions about:
- desired quantity;
- pace;
- capacity;
- service quality;
- mix;
- sustainability.


However exact portfolio allocation, quotas, priority tiers and operating-profile states belong to Director/Media/Operations/Architecture contracts unless Owner explicitly chooses otherwise. They should not be silently absorbed into the Golden Lead truth definition.


11. PREDICTION / SCORE POLICY


Any score, rank, probability or fit estimate is PREDICTION / DECISION SUPPORT — never Golden Lead Truth itself.


The historical `PREDICTED_GOLDEN_FIT_SCORE 0–100` is therefore RECLASSIFIED:
- useful historical candidate concept;
- not Core Truth;
- no fixed 0–100 requirement;
- no approved weights/thresholds;
- no global cross-business score.


If a prediction model exists later, it must preserve:
- predicted object/outcome;
- scope/horizon;
- evidence/features;
- confidence/uncertainty;
- missingness;
- model/calibration version;
- error costs;
- local validation status;
- prohibited use.


Prediction cannot override a verified local truth or constitutional gate.


12. TRUTH LABEL POLICY


Historical QUALITY / NOT_QUALITY / NOT_MEASURED is not automatically promoted as final canonical enum.


Strong retained principles:
- verified fundamental incompatibility may establish a negative fact within a defined dimension/scope;
- missing data does not establish negative quality;
- not-measured must remain distinguishable from fail;
- multidimensional truth should not be hidden behind one binary label when materially different dimensions remain unknown/mixed.


Exact label model is an Owner/Architecture decision.


13. ACTIONABILITY ≠ QUALITY


A high-value opportunity can be WAIT/NURTURE because timing is wrong.
A moderate current opportunity can require immediate action because a window is closing.
A lead may be hard to convert but valuable.
A lead may be easy to close but undesirable or low contribution.


Therefore:
FIT / VALUE / READINESS / WINDOW / CONVERSION DIFFICULTY / ACTIONABILITY / PRIORITY
must not be silently collapsed.


Actionability/priority mechanics primarily belong to Director/Sales/Media/Orchestration contracts.


14. DEVELOPABLE OPPORTUNITY


The historical `DEVELOPABLE_GOLDEN_OPPORTUNITY` contains a useful principle:
Some opportunities are not currently fully aligned but possess enough underlying fit/value that a justified, authorized intervention may improve realization or state.


Retain as Working concept, not mandatory Core entity.


The system must not present a developable opportunity as already-proven Golden Truth.


15. INTERVENTION BOUNDARY


Golden Lead may expose a value/realization gap; it does not automatically authorize a “transformation engine.”


Possible business-side changes include offer, route, qualification, response process, capacity, proof, decision access or timing.


Whether to intervene is a Director/Advisor/Domain/Authority decision considering:
- changeability;
- expected value;
- evidence;
- cost/time/effort;
- risk;
- opportunity cost;
- user goal;
- authority.


Not every non-Golden opportunity should be “fixed.”


16. LEAD-HANDLING CONNECTION


Sales/Lead Handling supplies evidence about:
- response/meaningful contact;
- qualification;
- progression;
- meeting/quote/proposal;
- no-show;
- won/lost/disqualified/no-response;
- handling effort;
- lost reasons;
- treatment changes.


Golden Lead uses these to distinguish potential from realization, not to blame Sales automatically or to equate close rate with quality.


17. MEASUREMENT CONNECTION


Metrics/Measurement defines what was actually measured, matching, coverage, clocks, maturity/censoring and causal limits.


Golden Lead cannot use:
- immature “not converted yet” as final negative;
- platform attributed conversion as business value truth;
- selection-biased survivor metrics as proof of predictor quality;
- CTR/CPL as Golden outcome by themselves.


18. LOCAL VALIDATION / CV-007+


Structural Golden Lead truth can be defined before CV-007+ empirical evidence.


CV-007+ is required for stronger use of:
- predictors;
- thresholds;
- weights;
- qualification signals;
- source/route/offer composition;
- response-time effects;
- economic relationships;
- cross-business transfer;
- Meta downstream optimization labels.


Pilot evidence calibrates operational models; it does not create the structural distinctions from scratch.


The identifier `CV-006` itself is ambiguous in current lineage and must be normalized before Build: historical structural B2B case and later “real Campainer won/lost empirical evidence” should receive distinct identifiers.


19. TRANSFERABILITY


Retain the transfer principle:
T0 structural distinctions may transfer broadly.
Mechanisms/prior directions require scope.
Predictors require target confirmation.
Thresholds/weights require local calibration.
Owner preferences/economics/capacity are local truth.
Realized outcomes belong to the target context.


Never auto-transfer:
- customer desirability;
- margin/capacity thresholds;
- response cutoff;
- qualification cutoff;
- demographic proxy;
- Golden score weights;
- CRM label meaning;
- Strategy/Route because another business looked similar.


Cross-business learning must not expose identifiable client/customer data to another client.


20. USER-FACING SEMANTICS


When relevant, Advisor should be able to distinguish for the user:
- what the business says it wants;
- what was measured here;
- what is predicted;
- what was learned elsewhere;
- what remains hypothesis/unknown;
- what actually happened after the lead arrived.


A user should not be told “this is a Golden Lead” with more certainty than the evidence supports.


21. PRESERVE THE BUSINESS SIDE OF THE NORTH STAR


Golden Lead is not a marketing-only concept.
The system must account for whether the business can responsibly accept and realize more of the desired opportunities.


More leads can reduce value when they overload handling/fulfillment, damage service, increase displacement or conflict with Desired Work.


Thus “more Golden Leads” means the quantity and pace appropriate to the business, not universal maximization.


22. DO-NOT-PROMOTE


Do not promote automatically:
- a global Golden Lead score;
- 0–100 score requirement;
- 50/30/20 or any weight formula;
- fixed binary QUALITY truth model as the only representation;
- universal fit/intent/readiness thresholds;
- owner-desired trait as targetable predictor;
- source/referral as Golden guarantee;
- close rate as Golden truth;
- won/lost as proof of acquisition quality without handling analysis;
- revenue/LTV/ROAS alone as Golden value;
- high propensity as high incremental opportunity;
- low uplift as low value/do-not-expose;
- industry similarity as transfer permission;
- historical opportunity-window categories/priority tiers/allocation quotas as Core Truth;
- “transformation engine” as automatic authority.

24. RECONCILIATION DISPOSITION


KEEP / CORE CANDIDATE:
- Golden Lead is contextual/relational and multidimensional;
- business/user desired value matters;
- Fit/Value/Serviceability/Economics/Opportunity state must not collapse;
- Potential Quality ≠ Business Realization ≠ Realized Outcome;
- Unknown ≠ Fail;
- Evidence+Confidence+Scope accompany inference;
- local business truth overrides cross-business prior when more direct/reliable;
- quantity/pace must respect capacity/sustainability;
- predictor/score ≠ truth.


EXPAND FROM CURRENT PROFESSIONAL KNOWLEDGE:
- explicit four-object Value/Propensity/Incremental Opportunity/Access split;
- Business Economics integration;
- Sales Handling realization separation;
- delayed-outcome/maturity guardrails;
- Desired Work as legitimate local value dimension;
- treatment-specific incremental opportunity.


SPLIT / MOVE OUT OF GOLDEN CORE:
- action priority engine → Director/operational contracts;
- resource allocation quotas/profiles → Director/Media/Economics;
- alert mechanics → Experience/Director/Sales/Metrics;
- nurture/retention runtime state machine → Sales/Orchestration/Experience;
- transformation/intervention execution → Director/Advisor/domain/authority;
- exact opportunity windows → domain/context/calibration.


KEEP WORKING:
- Developable Golden Opportunity;
- Golden envelope/view;
- coarse labels;
- prediction models;
- portfolio views.


REJECT AS CORE TRUTH:
- one global score;
- universal weights/cutoffs;
- behavior/intent/value conflation;
- realized sale as retroactive definition of potential quality;
- cross-business predictor auto-transfer.

Golden Lead domain is ready for canonical Core review at the structural level.

CV-007+ remains essential for predictor-level calibration and pilot learning, not a prerequisite for defining the Core’s structural truth.
