1. ROLE OF THE CORE


Metrics / Measurement Core מחזיק את האמת של מה נמדד, איך הוא מוגדר, מאיזה נתונים, באיזה Scope/Population/Window, באיזו איכות וביטחון, ומה מותר להסיק ממנו.


ה-Core מאפשר ל-Campainer:
- להבדיל Raw Data ממדד;
- להגדיר Metric/Outcome/Proxy במדויק;
- לשמור lineage מהמקור למשמעות;
- להפריד ביצוע מאיכות המדידה;
- להבחין Attribution מ-Incrementality;
- להבין maturity/censoring/missingness/matching;
- לחבר measurement ל-Golden Lead ולתוצאה עסקית;
- להבחין Observation/Prediction/Causal Evidence;
- לתעד Intervention/Concurrent Changes;
- להפיק evidence usable ל-Director/Advisor/Learning בלי להמציא Cause.


2. WHAT THIS CORE IS NOT


Metrics אינו:
- מטרת המוצר;
- Golden Lead Core;
- Director;
- Business Economics;
- Learning/Evolution promotion engine;
- Meta reporting UI;
- Dashboard;
- אוסף מספרים;
- רשות להחליט או לבצע;
- רשות להפוך metric movement לסיפור סיבתי.


Metric serves Truth and Decision. Metric is not the Goal.


3. ROOT PRINCIPLE — DECISION RELEVANCE


מספר/סיווג/מצב שאין בכוחו לשנות הבנה, החלטה, פעולה, מדידה או למידה אינו בהכרח Metric פעיל. הוא עשוי להיות Raw Data, diagnostic context או מידע שאין הצדקה לאסוף כרגע.


לכל מדד משמעותי צריך להיות ניתן לענות:
- איזו שאלה מקצועית הוא מודד;
- איזו החלטה עשויה להשתנות;
- מהו exact outcome/object;
- מה מקור הנתונים;
- מה population/denominator/unit/window;
- מה role: target / proxy / diagnostic / measurement-quality;
- מה מגבלות ההסקה;
- מה הקשר ל-Golden Lead/business outcome כאשר רלוונטי.


4. RAW DATA ≠ METRIC ≠ OUTCOME ≠ PROXY ≠ ESTIMAND


RAW DATA — אירוע/עובדה מקורית.
METRIC — הגדרה מקצועית מחושבת/מסווגת בתוך Scope ברור.
OUTCOME — התוצאה שהתרחשה ושאותה רוצים להבין/להעריך.
PROXY — מדד עקיף המייצג/מנבא/מתווך משהו עמוק יותר, ללא הנחה שהוא שקול לו.
ESTIMAND — הכמות/אפקט/שאלה המדויקת שאותה מנסים להעריך.


אין להחליף ביניהם בגלל נוחות פלטפורמה.


5. METRIC CONTRACT — CANDIDATE CORE PRINCIPLE


Metric משמעותי צריך לשמור, ברמת העיקרון:
- identity/name;
- professional question;
- semantic definition;
- unit;
- population/eligibility;
- numerator/denominator when relevant;
- data source(s);
- time/window/horizon;
- scope;
- target/proxy/diagnostic role;
- known exclusions/missingness;
- measurement quality/confidence;
- decisions supported;
- limitations/prohibited inference;
- baseline/benchmark/target if applicable;
- freshness/reopen condition.


Exact field schema remains Architecture.


6. ATOMIC / COMPOSITE METRICS


The historical recursive metric-graph principle is retained:
- composite metrics must expose components;
- computational relationships must be distinguishable from diagnostic/catalog relationships;
- no double counting;
- no circular metric dependency;
- no summary score may hide a mandatory failure or missingness.


Atomicity is professional, not technical: stop decomposing when further decomposition no longer improves decision/diagnosis/action/measurement meaning.


7. PERFORMANCE ≠ MEASUREMENT QUALITY


שיפור coverage, matching, logging, attribution confidence או sample size אינו שיפור בקמפיין/עסק.


Measurement quality tells us how well we know performance.
It does not artificially raise or lower performance.


Candidate measurement-quality dimensions include:
- source reliability;
- coverage;
- matching/identity quality;
- missingness;
- freshness;
- sample/precision;
- outcome maturity/censoring;
- consistency/triangulation;
- assignment/exposure integrity where experimental;
- causal identification strength.


No single measurement-confidence score is required by Core.


8. MISSING / PENDING / CENSORED ≠ ZERO / NEGATIVE


Not measured, missing, not yet matured, censored or unmatched states must remain explicit.


An opportunity that has not converted yet is not automatically a negative label.
A missing CRM field is not a failed outcome.
A blank source is not zero.


Outcome maturity is target/business/Offering specific; no universal maturity window is promoted.


9. LEADING SIGNAL ≠ BUSINESS OUTCOME


CTR, view rate, click, form completion, appointment booking, response time and other leading/process indicators may be useful.
They are not business success by themselves.


The measurement chain may include:
Exposure/Engagement
→ Lead
→ Contact
→ Qualification
→ Meeting/Consultation/Quote/Opportunity
→ Won/Lost
→ Contribution/Collection
→ Fulfillment/Cancellation/Rework
→ Repeat/Cross-Buy/Referral where relevant.


Not every business requires every stage. Exact stage semantics are local/domain contracts.


10. LEAD POTENTIAL ≠ REALIZATION ≠ OUTCOME


Metrics must preserve the cross-domain distinction:
LEAD POTENTIAL QUALITY
≠ BUSINESS REALIZATION CAPABILITY/TREATMENT
≠ REALIZED OUTCOME.


Sales handling, response, qualification and follow-up may affect observed outcomes and labels.
Therefore “lost” does not automatically mean “bad acquisition”; “won” does not automatically prove strong potential predictor.


11. ATTRIBUTION ≠ INCREMENTALITY


Attribution answers who/what receives credit under a reporting rule/model.
Incrementality asks what changed because of an intervention relative to a counterfactual.


Platform-attributed conversion/ROAS is valid operational evidence within its semantics, not automatic causal proof.


Before/after alone is not causal proof.
Exposed vs unexposed observation is not automatically randomized evidence.
Prediction is not treatment effect.


12. OBSERVATION ≠ EXPERIMENT


Evidence classes may include observational, attribution, quasi-experimental and randomized evidence.
Core principle: causal language must match the design/integrity actually achieved.


Randomization helps causal identification but does not guarantee:
- precision;
- correct implementation;
- clean exposure;
- no interference;
- no attrition/missingness;
- external validity.


“A/B test” is a label, not proof that assignment/logging/analysis are valid.


13. ASSIGNMENT ≠ EXPOSURE


Where experiments are used, being assigned to treatment is not identical to being exposed/receiving it.


Measurement must preserve the relevant treatment/exposure semantics and contamination/interference risks.


Exact experiment architecture remains Measurement Professional Knowledge / Build, not Core truth.


14. SELECTION / SURVIVOR BIAS


Conditioning only on survivors after qualification, response, contact, click or another post-treatment stage can distort inference.


Examples:
- higher survivor close rate can coexist with lower total value due false negatives;
- CRM “qualified” may reflect human treatment/process, not latent lead quality;
- campaign audiences delivered by an algorithm may have selection effects.


Metrics must preserve eligible denominator and selection path where decision-relevant.


15. BASELINE SNAPSHOT / CONCURRENT CHANGE


Before interpreting a material change, measurement should be able to reference the relevant pre-change Baseline Snapshot and concurrent interventions/business changes.


Potential concurrent changes include:
- campaign/media;
- creative;
- offer/pricing;
- route;
- CRM/measurement;
- lead handling;
- staffing/capacity;
- fulfillment;
- market/seasonality/external event.


Result After Change ≠ Result Caused By Change.


Complete User Context owns the local state/change history; Metrics owns the measurement interpretation and causal limitations.


16. INTERVENTION LINEAGE


A material intervention/test should be traceable conceptually through:
Problem/Decision Need
→ Evidence/Hypothesis
→ Actor/Authority
→ Treatment/Change
→ Assignment/Exposure where relevant
→ Primary Outcome
→ Downstream/Guardrail Outcomes
→ Alternative Explanations/Threats
→ Result + Uncertainty
→ Preserve/Change/Rollback/Reopen.


Exact lineage schema is Architecture/Learning/Measurement interface work.


17. DEEPEST RELIABLE OUTCOME


“Measure the deepest possible outcome” is not a universal rule.


Deeper outcomes are often more business-relevant but may be delayed, sparse, poorly matched or semantically unstable.


For each decision:
Prefer the deepest outcome that is reliable enough to support that decision, while retaining deeper outcomes for later evaluation when they mature.


Optimization target ≠ evaluation target by necessity.


18. CREATIVE JOB MEASUREMENT


Creative success is not CTR alone.


Measurement should preserve:
CREATIVE/STRATEGIC JOB
→ expected STATE CHANGE / mechanism indicator
→ downstream business progression
→ incremental effect only when design supports it.


STATE_INDICATOR ≠ DOWNSTREAM_BUSINESS_OUTCOME ≠ INCREMENTAL_EFFECT.


GAP-012 remains calibration work; the structural measurement separation is ready.


19. BUSINESS ECONOMICS CONNECTION


Metrics consumes exact business-economic definitions; it does not invent them.


Revenue ≠ Contribution.
Booked ≠ Collected.
Average ≠ Marginal.
Attributed value ≠ Incremental value.


Where campaign/business decisions depend on economics, outcome contracts must specify the relevant economic object/horizon and maturity.


20. BASELINE PROPENSITY ≠ INCREMENTAL OPPORTUNITY


Metrics must keep separate:
- baseline outcome propensity;
- observed/predicted customer value;
- incremental marketing opportunity/treatment effect;
- access/retrievability need.


Hillstrom evidence supports this separation but does not supply Meta effect sizes or local Golden truth.


21. BENCHMARKS / TARGETS / THRESHOLDS


Benchmark ≠ Metric ≠ Goal ≠ Threshold.


Preferred comparison hierarchy depends on decision and availability; useful references may include:
- same-business baseline;
- explicit local target;
- historical period;
- trend;
- comparable Offering/campaign in same business;
- controlled test;
- carefully scoped external prior.


Industry/platform benchmark alone is weak contextual prior, not local truth.


No universal sample-size, duration, MDE, p-value, match-rate, coverage or response-time threshold is promoted.


22. PRESERVE / WAIT / INCONCLUSIVE


Measurement may validly support:
- CHANGE;
- PRESERVE WHAT WORKS;
- WAIT for outcome maturity;
- COLLECT more evidence;
- INCONCLUSIVE / UNKNOWN;
- REOPEN.


“Inconclusive” is a valid scientific/professional result, not a system failure.


23. USER-FACING CAUSAL LANGUAGE


Advisor must not say “X caused Y” when measurement supports only association/attribution.


User-facing language should reflect evidence class and confidence, with distinctions such as observed/associated/promising/causally-supported/inconclusive where appropriate.


Exact approved vocabulary/thresholds require Owner decision and Experience/Advisor reconciliation.


24. MEASUREMENT FRESHNESS


Reopen measurement contracts/evidence when material semantics or data-generating process change, including:
- CRM label definitions;
- route;
- qualification/sales process;
- Offering/price;
- Meta capability/delivery;
- identity/matching;
- capacity/fulfillment;
- measurement coverage;
- market/seasonality;
- model/drift;
- contradiction/replication failure.


25. METRIC DEVELOPMENT / DEMOTION


Metric, proxy, threshold, formula or weight is testable and revisable.


Changes must preserve:
- before state;
- rationale/hypothesis;
- evidence;
- changed semantics/formula/threshold;
- expected impact;
- countermetrics/guardrails;
- outcome after change;
- rollback/reopen;
- governance when changing canonical truth.


Failed metrics are retained as lineage; no silent rewrite.


26. RELATION TO KNOWLEDGE / EVIDENCE


Metrics produces Measurement/Evidence objects with exact semantics and limitations.
Knowledge/Evidence governs claim/evidence status and professional use.


Metric maturity is not Knowledge truth promotion.


27. RELATION TO GOLDEN LEAD


Golden Lead defines multidimensional business value/fit truth.
Metrics defines how dimensions/outcomes are observed/measured and what confidence/coverage exists.


Metrics must not recreate a hidden global Golden score merely to simplify reporting.


28. RELATION TO DIRECTOR / ADVISOR


Director receives decision-specific measurement evidence, uncertainty, target/proxy distinction and causal limits.
Advisor interprets and explains the result professionally.


Neither may rewrite metric semantics to defend a previous decision.


29. RELATION TO LEARNING


Metrics supplies reviewed result/evidence and intervention lineage.
Learning/Evolution decides how that evidence creates/updates Learning Candidates, scope, promotion/demotion/reopen.


A result does not self-promote a rule.


30. RELATION TO META / MEDIA


Meta/Media provide actual delivery/experiment capabilities, assignment/exposure/logging sources and operational metrics.
Metrics determines what those measurements mean and what may be inferred.


Current Meta causal/experiment capability is freshness-sensitive and requires live validation before pilot use.


31. DO-NOT-PROMOTE


Do not promote automatically:
- platform attribution as incrementality;
- before/after as causal proof;
- A/B label as experiment integrity;
- exposed-vs-unexposed as randomized evidence;
- proxy win as Golden/business win;
- pending outcome as negative;
- CRM label as latent truth;
- revenue as contribution;
- one global Golden score;
- measurement confidence as performance;
- universal sample size/duration/MDE/p-value rule;
- universal match/coverage cutoff;
- fixed response-time threshold;
- external effect size as local expectation;
- Working metric/lineage schema as final Runtime schema.
