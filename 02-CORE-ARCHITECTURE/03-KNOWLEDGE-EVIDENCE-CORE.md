0. PURPOSE


מסמך זה הוא תוצר Wave A של Truth/Core Reconciliation לאחר קבלת חבילת ה-Professional Knowledge המלאה. מטרתו אינה לקדם אמת קנונית אוטומטית, אלא לנסח את אמת-התחום המועמדת של Knowledge / Evidence כך שניתן יהיה להעביר אותה לביקורת בעל המוצר ולהשתמש בה כבסיס לארכיטקטורה רק לאחר הכרעה מפורשת.


המסמך יורש את CAMPAINER-CONSTITUTION-001 v1.1 ואת CAMPAINER-PRODUCT-TRUTH-CORE-001 v1.2. הוא אינו משנה אותם.


1. ROLE OF THE CORE


Knowledge / Evidence Core מחזיק את המשטר המקצועי של מה Campainer יודעת, על סמך מה, באיזה Scope, באיזו רמת בשלות/ביטחון, מה סותר אותה, מה עדיין חסר, מתי הידע רלוונטי, ומה מותר או אסור להסיק ממנו.


ה-Core אינו:
- מאגר כל הידע המקצועי עצמו.
- Complete User Context.
- Director.
- Advisor.
- Metrics Core.
- Learning / Evolution Core.
- Search/RAG implementation.
- Prompt library.
- Runtime schema.
- סמכות ביצוע.


2. ROOT DISTINCTIONS — CANDIDATE CORE TRUTH


2.1 Source ≠ Evidence ≠ Claim ≠ Truth.
מקור הוא חומר שממנו ניתן להפיק ראיה; ראיה היא תמיכה/החלשה/הקשר לטענה; Claim הוא ניסוח על המציאות; Truth הוא מעמד מחייב רק כאשר ה-Governance המתאים העניק לו מעמד זה.


2.2 Prior ≠ Local Truth.
Research, industry knowledge, cross-business learning, benchmarks, historical cases and professional patterns may generate priors, hypotheses, questions and decision support. They do not become facts about the current business without suitable local evidence.


2.3 Prediction ≠ Truth ≠ Realized Outcome.
Score, probability, ranking, model output or recommendation never changes the epistemic status of reality.


2.4 Unknown ≠ Zero ≠ Fail.
Missing, not measured, immature, inaccessible or not-yet-observed data remain explicit states. They are not silently converted to zero or failure.


2.5 Observation ≠ Cause; Correlation ≠ Causation.
Causal language requires a design/evidence path that supports it.


2.6 Maturity ≠ Authority.
A highly mature Knowledge Item may be useful for decision support without becoming Product Truth, Build Authority or Runtime Authority.


2.7 Validation ≠ Canonical Promotion.
Empirical support can change Knowledge Maturity and Confidence. Canonical promotion requires the appropriate Truth/Owner governance path.


2.8 Platform Guidance ≠ Marketing Truth.
Platform capability, constraint, guidance and published evidence retain their own type and freshness.


2.9 Cross-business evidence ≠ target-business fact.
Transfer requires explicit scope and local validation proportional to the decision risk.


2.10 Contradiction is a Knowledge Asset.
Contradictions are preserved, scoped and investigated; they are not deleted to produce a clean model.


3. KNOWLEDGE ITEM — CONCEPTUAL UNIT


The target conceptual unit is Knowledge Item. This is not a final technical schema.


A material Knowledge Item should be representable through at least:
- identity / statement;
- type;
- source / provenance / lineage;
- applicability scope;
- supporting evidence;
- weakening / contradicting evidence;
- limitations / prohibited inference;
- confidence / maturity;
- freshness / recheck state;
- consumer roles;
- activation conditions;
- permitted uses;
- prohibited uses;
- local validation need;
- reopen / demotion / retirement trigger.


The purpose is decision-aware retrieval and activation, not document retrieval alone.


4. EPISTEMIC STATES — CANDIDATE CORE MODEL


Campainer must be able to distinguish at least the following semantic states where relevant:
TRUTH / LOCAL FACT / EVIDENCE / OBSERVATION / PRIOR / PATTERN / HYPOTHESIS / PREDICTION / INFERENCE / MEASUREMENT / UNKNOWN / CONTRADICTED / STALE.


This list is semantic, not a final enum. Architecture may normalize names, but it may not collapse materially different states when doing so changes what may be inferred or done.


5. EVIDENCE DISCIPLINE


Evidence is evaluated relative to the claim and decision scope, not by source prestige alone.


Material evidence review should consider:
- quality;
- directness to claim;
- scope fit;
- recency/freshness;
- independence;
- replicability/triangulation;
- known bias and missingness;
- counterevidence;
- what the source does not establish.


A high-quality general paper can be indirect to a local business claim. A local CRM record can be highly direct but measurement-poor. Both facts must survive synthesis.


Evidence may SUPPORT / WEAKEN / CONTRADICT / CONTEXTUALIZE / remain NEUTRAL relative to a claim. Exact labels are not Core truth; preservation of direction and limitations is.


6. KNOWLEDGE MATURITY — DECISION READINESS, NOT TRUTH SCORE


The existing M0–M5 ladder is retained as a strong Working model, not frozen as the only future enum:
M0 UNASSESSED
M1 SOURCE IDENTIFIED
M2 SUPPORTED PRIOR
M3 TRIANGULATED PATTERN
M4 DECISION-SUPPORT READY
M5 REPLICATED IN SCOPE


Core-level principle:
Higher maturity means greater justified professional usability inside a defined scope. It does not mean universal truth, authority to act, or immunity from later demotion.


7. KNOWLEDGE GAP


A Knowledge Gap is not simply “something we do not know.” It becomes a managed work object when resolving it can materially change a decision, reduce meaningful risk, improve measurement, improve Golden Lead judgment, or change an intervention.


For a material Gap, Campainer should be able to know:
- what question is open;
- which decision is affected;
- what current prior exists, if any;
- what is missing;
- best evidence route;
- cost of learning: time, money, user load, privacy, risk and opportunity cost;
- whether work may continue with the gap open;
- interim rule;
- expected closure/review output;
- status and reopen condition.


Principle: do not ask or collect merely to know more. Seek the smallest/best evidence that can improve the decision.


8. CONTRADICTION HANDLING


Before resolving an apparent contradiction, inspect:
- definition;
- scope;
- population/context;
- time/version;
- measurement;
- method;
- authority/role;
- ethical/legal boundary.


Valid dispositions include:
UNRESOLVED / SCOPE SPLIT / BOTH CONTEXTUAL / LATER SOURCE SUPERSEDES / EVIDENCE FAVORS A / EVIDENCE FAVORS B / NEEDS TEST.


No requirement exists that every contradiction end with one universal winner.


9. ACTIVATION


Knowledge that cannot explain when it becomes relevant remains a library, not a product capability.


A Knowledge family should be activatable by:
- current decision need;
- stage/moment;
- local context;
- trigger signal or event;
- relevant unknown/contradiction;
- consumer role;
- freshness state;
- risk/authority boundary.


Activation does not mean automatic adoption. It means “bring this knowledge into the decision context for consideration.”


10. ROLE PROJECTION


The same Knowledge Item may serve multiple roles, but each role should receive the minimum sufficient authorized projection for its decision.


Examples:
Advisor — synthesis, alternatives, uncertainty, explanation.
Creative — strategic/meaning constraints, message job, proof need and relevant evidence without replacing creative judgment.
Media/Budget — experiment role, economic context, uncertainty and marginal decision inputs.
Meta/Technical — capability/executability/permission/constraint inputs.
Sales/Lead Handling — qualification object, next-step intent, handling/feedback semantics.
Metrics/Learning — measurement meaning, exposure/treatment, outcome, causal risk, transferability and reopen logic.


Projection is governed by purpose, relevance and permission, not by “role title gets full file.”


11. LOCAL VALIDATION / TRANSFERABILITY


Working transfer ladder retained for architecture normalization:
T0 Structure — broadly transferable ontology/distinction.
T1 Mechanism — scoped mechanism prior.
T2 Directional Prior — hypothesis/question generator.
T3 Predictor — requires target confirmation.
T4 Threshold / Weight / Cutoff — local calibration.
T5 Business Truth — target-local only.
T6 Realized Outcome — target-local evidence.


Core principle: the more a Knowledge Item moves from structure toward prediction, thresholds, business truth and outcomes, the stronger the local-validation requirement.


No industry label, similarity score or cross-business frequency automatically promotes T1/T2 to T5.


12. FRESHNESS / RECHECK


Freshness is part of knowledge, not metadata decoration.


Recheck requirements depend on the knowledge mechanism and decision horizon. No universal refresh interval is imposed.


Particularly freshness-sensitive families include:
- platform capability/guidance/policy;
- permissions/integrations;
- market/regulatory context;
- economics/prices/costs;
- capacity;
- brand asset rights/availability;
- external signal access/API availability;
- local process and sales handling state.


A stale item must not silently behave as current truth.


13. PROMOTION / DEMOTION / REOPEN


Knowledge can mature, split, be scoped, demoted, reopened or retired.


Promotion within the professional library requires evidence appropriate to the claim and decision scope. It never grants itself Canonical, Build or Runtime authority.


Demotion/reopen triggers include:
- material contradictory evidence;
- failure to replicate;
- platform change;
- changed business/Offering/context;
- measurement flaw discovered;
- scope overreach;
- drift;
- local evidence that conflicts with the prior;
- expired permissions or provenance concern.


No Silent Promotion. No Silent Completion. No Silent Supersession.


14. RELATION TO COMPLETE USER CONTEXT


Knowledge/Evidence owns general/scoped professional knowledge and its evidence semantics.
Complete User Context owns the living local truth/history of the specific user/business/Offering/account/context.


A user statement may be Local Evidence or Local Truth depending on the object and authority. Example: the owner can be authoritative about a chosen business goal; the same owner is not automatically authoritative about a buyer's latent motivation.


Knowledge does not absorb the user file. Context does not turn every local observation into general knowledge.


15. RELATION TO DIRECTOR / ADVISOR


Knowledge/Evidence supplies relevant truth-state, evidence, priors, gaps, contradictions and limitations.
Director uses the appropriate projection to frame and choose the decision state.
Advisor exercises professional judgment, recommends and explains to the user.


Knowledge does not choose because it knows; Advisor/Director do not fabricate facts because a decision is needed.


16. RELATION TO METRICS / LEARNING


Metrics owns measurement meaning and what was actually measured.
Learning/Evolution owns the controlled path from result/evidence to candidate learning, validation, promotion/demotion and propagation.


Knowledge/Evidence owns the epistemic representation of claims and supporting/contradicting evidence after they enter the knowledge system.


Observed result → Evidence/Candidate does not become a generalized rule without Learning governance.


17. EXTERNAL SIGNALS BOUNDARY


External Signals are sources/observations used for market intelligence. They do not become person-level intent truth merely because they are public or predictive.


Public ≠ unrestricted.
Observable ≠ permitted to store.
Storage ≠ permission to target.
Research access ≠ commercial access.


External Signal interpretation must retain source, scope, time, bias, freshness, alternative explanations and permission boundary.


18. SEMANTIC HANDOFF BOUNDARY


Knowledge/Evidence participates in Semantic Handoff by preserving meaning, evidence, uncertainty, source and scope in projections.


It does not own every professional interface. Exact Advisor↔Creative, Creative↔Production and other domain contracts remain separate open architecture work.


19. DO-NOT-PROMOTE


Do not promote automatically:
- document title/status into truth;
- source count into confidence;
- benchmark into local threshold;
- platform guidance into hard rule;
- one case into general law;
- prediction into fact;
- association into cause;
- industry pattern into business truth;
- structural case validation into performance proof;
- maturity into authority;
- absence of evidence into zero/failure;
- cross-business pattern into local predictor/weight;
- historical hard counts/taxonomies into closed ontology.
