1. Purpose and authority boundary
This document aligns the current build-package set to the living Campainer Product Truth and Core Architecture corpus. It does not rewrite package history, create a new core or domain, grant Build Authority, accept F0, close ER-016, or authorize code/runtime mutation.
For implementation and review, the effective requirement for each build package is: its current package version + this alignment + the current canonical sources named below. Where a lower source conflicts with a higher source, the higher source controls.
* Current canonical Campainer Product Truth, Core Architecture, Shared Experience, Stage Truth, Contracts, and promoted owner-approved architecture dispositions
* This CURRENT ARCHITECTURE ALIGNMENT — 001
* The current version of the specific BP-00…BP-12 package
* Older indices, superseded versions, candidates, drafts, and reference-only returns
2. Non-negotiable architecture baseline
* Preserve the established 13-core and 10-domain topology. No package may invent a shadow core, shared brain, global truth store, super-advisor, or new decision owner.
* Expose one user-facing Advisor identity. Product-owned professional specialists remain bounded contributors; they do not become additional user-facing advisors.
* Keep Authority separate from capability and execution; Evidence separate from Truth; Local Context separate from Knowledge; Measurement separate from causality and Learning.
* Every governed meaning has one explicit owner. Cross-boundary movement uses semantic handoff/return contracts and minimum-sufficient projections, not raw store sharing.
* Knowledge-item activation, projection, and usage must remain traceable. External research, benchmarks, and priors are evidence or calibration inputs, never silent local truth.
* Keep observed opportunity, validated opportunity, strategic choice, and execution plan distinct. No shadow decisions or implicit promotion paths.
* Campaign copy remains a semantic projection through the existing C06/C07 path. Do not create a persisted Campaign Copy Decision object, core, or domain.
3. Cross-cutting current architecture requirements
3.1 Shared Experience and Advisor Workspace
* Use a white-first shared workspace with a right-side RTL product navigation that supports expanded, compact, and hidden/drawer states.
* Provide a left Advisor panel with closed, compact, contextual, resized, and dedicated states. The central work surface must reflow; it must not be hidden by overlays.
* Support contextual expand-in-place and a dedicated Advisor Workspace with an approximate one-third Advisor / two-thirds work split; treat the ratio as responsive guidance, not a rigid pixel contract.
* Provide Universal Advisor Entry and Direct UI as first-class routes over the same governed objects, truth, scope, and permissions.
* Preserve scope, filters, drafts, work state, and return context across transitions. Never mix businesses, accounts, workspaces, or unauthorized product context.
* Represent Fact, Unknown, Hypothesis, Recommendation, Decision, Action, Result, and System Status with distinct accessible grammar. Do not rely on color alone.
* For shared-product material, show product ownership, source, freshness, and attribution. Partial or unavailable states must be explicit.
3.2 Deep Diagnosis and ER-014 promotion
* Deep Diagnosis is Stage 4; Full Strategy is Stage 5. The existing C05/C06 boundaries remain intact.
* Enforce a durable cumulative maximum of 15 minutes before Creative Studio. The budget survives retries, aliases, versions, branch changes, and resumed sessions.
* Diagnosis must produce four governed outcomes: Business Structure, overall Potential Advertising Structure, current advertising structure, and current campaign.
* Every closed-answer question exposes visible Other + free text + Unknown/Not decided. Free-text gaps trigger targeted follow-up, not a full restart.
* Non-blocking missing information becomes a governed dashboard question with a relevance gate and a due-before condition.
* There is no raw Diagnosis-to-Creative bypass. Rapid Market & Creative Scan remains deferred and non-canonical.
3.3 D-44 shared-product collaboration and durable learning
* Use one user-facing conversation with two product-owned professional specialists, ephemeral neutral coordination, federated source-owned knowledge, linked local decisions/learning, and receiver-owned action authority.
* Do not create a shared brain, shared truth/runtime/permission layer, third advisor, super-advisor, global decision, or automatic cross-product action authority.
* Support UNIFIED, CAMPAINER_ONLY, LEADER_ONLY, SEPARATE_VIEWS, and EXCLUDE_PRODUCT_DATA modes with explicit consent, scope, and failure isolation.
* Conversation coordination is ephemeral. Product Truth and Evidence remain local. Durable federated learning exchange is a separate governed plane.
* Signal received is not a local learning candidate; a candidate is not promoted learning; promoted learning is not Product Truth. Independent local reconciliation is mandatory.
* Use source-owned, minimum-necessary projections with source product, version, freshness, attribution, retention, consent, and correction/revocation semantics.
* On disconnect or staleness, mark source disconnected, age freshness, revalidate or demote dependent learning, reopen affected decisions when required, and apply governed deletion/retention behavior.
* T1 remains narrow and read-only. Cross-product actions, if later introduced, move intent only and remain subject to receiver-owned local authorization.
3.4 Advisor R0–R8 promotion
* Preserve the canonical v3.7 strategy baseline and treat the promoted v3.8 material as an additive delta only.
* Route adopted content through existing Strategy, Knowledge/Evidence, Context, Brand, Creative, Experience, Sales/CRM, Measurement, Learning, and Economics ownership.
* Rapid Scan stays deferred; P1-C03 is not launched; commercial entitlements remain separate from product architecture and truth.
