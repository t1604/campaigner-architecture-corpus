OWNER-APPROVED ENTITLEMENT LIFECYCLE ADDENDUM — 2026-08-19
Cancellation / refund / chargeback do not erase completed professional work and do not rewrite historical Truth, Evidence, Review, Authority or Lineage.
They may suspend or terminate entitlement for future professional work not yet performed, according to the current governed entitlement state.
If entitlement changes while material professional work is in progress, no new material stage, irreversible cost or new external commitment may begin until the entitlement state is resolved by the appropriate Human/Commercial Review.
Chargeback or payment dispute does not create, revoke or retroactively rewrite live-action Authorization, Human Review or execution history. It changes only the entitlement to continue professional work.
Every entitlement-state change must be auditable, scope-bound and version-bound, and must preserve the exact commercial event/source that caused it.
Payment/entitlement state remains distinct from Approval, Authorization, Execution and Verified State.
Exact monetary refund formula, fees, legal terms, provider-specific dispute mechanics and final payment-state enums remain Deferred and are not silently inferred by Build.
