OWNER-APPROVED ADDENDUM — END CONDITION / EXTENSION / DEFAULT CLOSURE
Owner Decision source: CAMPAINER-F5-END-CONDITION-CLOSURE-OWNER-DECISION-001 — APPROVED — ID 1LXPly_A-eqHalvr8MwcG8Kdm1wPcooufipdhKFcUOXg.


1. The One-Time user defines the approved campaign envelope in advance, including the media-budget cap and any hard date/event boundary that limits how long the campaign may remain live.
2. Campainer must notify the user before expected end so the user can explicitly choose whether to extend. Exact notification timing/cadence/channel remain Build/Experience details.
3. Silence/no response never authorizes extension.
4. Without an explicitly approved/authorized extension, the campaign closes when the approved media-budget cap is exhausted.
5. If an explicit hard date/event boundary occurs earlier, the campaign must not remain live beyond that boundary merely because budget remains.
6. Any extension that changes budget/time/scope/objective/material meaning is a new material decision and must re-enter the applicable approval/authorization/Human Review/Fresh Preflight chain.
7. Deterministic mechanical closure in the pilot is allowed only for an already-approved exact budget cap or an objectively testable hard timestamp/event deadline. Other classes such as sold/rented, inventory exhausted, capacity exhausted, business objective achieved or responsible-stop may trigger closure review but do not automatically mint closure authority unless a later governed contract explicitly makes that class deterministic.
8. Ambiguous/contradictory/stale/insufficient End Condition evidence never silently extends and never claims certainty; route to Human Review/Semantic Return/pending closure as applicable while preserving any hard pre-approved boundary.
9. Actual closure remains a protected external action through the applicable BP-08 execution/readback chain. Provider receipt alone is not verified closure.


OWNER-APPROVED ADDENDUM — USER-INITIATED EARLY STOP / UNUSED MEDIA BALANCE
Owner Decision source: CAMPAINER-ONE-TIME-UNUSED-MEDIA-BALANCE-OWNER-DECISION-001 — APPROVED / LEGAL-FINANCE RELEASE GATE — ID 1-kiC1QKNgeqwpuKdfC_cto8J1JwFz-wAAaYqNKiJF7Q.
Related hard invariant: CAMPAINER-MEDIA-FUNDING-NO-CREDIT-EXPOSURE-OWNER-DECISION-001 — ID 1VMKuxBFX7nJNG5_wh7-cRyE_irEojEEAr8tCu5V4HVY.


10. User-initiated stop before budget exhaustion is a valid closure trigger and does not imply automatic refund, forfeiture or Campainer revenue recognition.
11. After BP-08 protected stop + verified closure + final spend/readback, the system must determine all settled/unsettled Meta exposure and lock the required media-coverage floor before calculating any eligible unused remainder.
12. Eligible unused media remainder moves to a segregated, exact-user/exact-One-Time-Scope identified media balance with source-payment/campaign/final-spend/settlement lineage.
13. The residual balance cannot be mixed with service fees, AI/management revenue, another user/business/campaign, or silently reused. Funding Balance ≠ Spending Authorization.
14. During the applicable safety/claim period, the user may request return of the eligible balance. Refund execution must preserve sufficient coverage for all Meta exposure/lag/reversal/dispute obligations.
15. The safety/claim period begins only after verified closure and sufficiently known Meta exposure/settlement state, not merely when Stop is requested.
16. Product Owner intent is that after the approved safety/claim period, if no refund request was made, the remaining balance may become releasable to Campainer. This release step is blocked by a mandatory Legal/Finance gate: exact lawful waiting period, contractual disclosure/consent, consumer/payment-services/unclaimed-funds treatment, required notices, accounting/tax and licensing/segregation must be approved before implementation or revenue recognition.
17. Until that Legal/Finance gate is closed, elapsed time alone cannot convert the balance into Campainer revenue.
18. A media-funding dispute/chargeback removes the disputed amount from spendable coverage and can require hold/stop; it cannot accelerate or authorize transfer of the residual balance to Campainer.
19. Reuse of a residual balance for a later campaign requires explicit user instruction plus the applicable new Media/Budget decision/authorization.
20. Closure evidence must include final Meta exposure, protected coverage floor, eligible residual amount, balance identity, refund-request state and Legal/Finance release-gate state.
