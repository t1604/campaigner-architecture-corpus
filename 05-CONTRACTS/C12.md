Applies
Within A10 and B7 whenever management recommends a material live/business change.


Trigger
Live evidence produces a Recommendation/Test/Task/Allocation/Creative/Business Intervention decision.


Producer
Director + applicable professional domain + Advisor.


Receiver
User/authority owner and, after authorization, EO or business task owner.


Must Carry
Current state, decision trigger, evidence/maturity, recommended change, expected mechanism, budget/resource effect, risks/uncertainty, what remains unchanged, measurement intent, and reopen/rollback conditions when material.


Must Not Carry / Forbidden
Recommendation/test decision must not authorize a live change by itself; requested change must not silently exceed original meaning or evidence.


Authority / Receiver Discretion
Execution occurs only after the correct approval/authorization for the action class. Business tasks may be owned by the user/business; live platform changes route to EO only after authority.


Required Output
Authorized change, user/business task, or explicit no-change/Wait/Preserve state.


Semantic Return
Return to the owning domain if requested change exceeds original meaning or evidence.


Evidence / Lineage
Decision → authority → intervention → outcome linkage.


Invariant
Managed recommendation ≠ live authority; material change must re-enter the authority chain.


Common Envelope
Identity/Scope, Intent/Meaning, Decision Need, minimum-sufficient Local Context, Knowledge/Evidence/Unknowns, Freshness, Authority State, Protected Constraints, Receiver Discretion, Required Output/Return and Lineage apply when relevant. Exact fields/enums/API remain Deferred.


Pilot Human Review
Material professional/execution decisions remain under 100% Human Review according to applicable Stage Truth and pilot policy.
