Applies
Any material handoff.


Trigger
Receiver cannot preserve intent, scope, truth, authority or protected constraints; required evidence is missing; capability/freshness invalidates the path; receiver discovers a materially different problem; or proposed fallback changes meaning.


Producer
Receiving Core/Stage.


Receiver
Owner of broken meaning/decision.


Must Carry
Original request/intent and Scope; what remains valid; what failed/is missing/contradicted/stale; evidence/capability basis; what cannot be assumed; whether current authorization remains valid, narrowed or unusable; next decision owner; and safe alternatives if known without making the upstream decision.


Must Not Carry / Forbidden
Return must not silently substitute a new Strategy, audience, budget, Brand truth, claim, measurement target or action.


Authority / Receiver Discretion
The receiver does not gain authority to repair broken meaning by substitution. It must preserve valid state, surface the break and return to the correct decision owner.


Required Output
Explicit Semantic Return with preserved-valid state, broken element, basis and next decision owner.


Semantic Return
This contract is itself the governed Return path; the returned issue routes to the owner of broken meaning.


Evidence / Lineage
Return event/decision lineage.


Invariant
When meaning cannot be preserved, Return is mandatory; no Shadow Decision.


Common Envelope
Identity/Scope, Intent/Meaning, Decision Need, minimum-sufficient Local Context, Knowledge/Evidence/Unknowns, Freshness, Authority State, Protected Constraints, Receiver Discretion, Required Output/Return and Lineage apply when relevant. Exact fields/enums/API remain Deferred.


Pilot Human Review
Material professional/execution decisions remain under 100% Human Review according to applicable Stage Truth and pilot policy.
